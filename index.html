Help & FAQ

v2.1 • quick start and common questions
Start simple

You do not have to configure everything at once.

Begin with four commitments, one or two meaningful goals, a few minutes of reflection, and adjust the system as your practice becomes clearer.

Quick start

  1. Run the optional setup wizard, or skip it.
  2. Use Today for My 3 and activity logging.
  3. Use Journal for intention, free writing and evening reflection.
  4. Keep at least two mental and two physical Commitments.
  5. Create discrete Goals with dates and milestones.
  6. Use Meditation for chant, ocean breath or soft bell.
  7. Use Wisdom to read, watch and save useful resources.
  8. Review Progress weekly, then adjust rather than judge.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my information stored?
This version is local-first. Your journal, goals, commitments and logs are stored in this browser on this device. There is no account or cloud database in this build.
How do I protect or move my data?
Go to Progress and use Backup data to export a full JSON backup. CSV export provides a simpler activity record. Keep backups somewhere safe before clearing browser data or changing devices.
What is the 2 mental + 2 physical rule?
It is the baseline structure: at least two commitments for the mind / inner life and two for the body / physical life. You can rename them, change metrics, add more, and add sub-branches underneath them.
What are sub-branches?
Sub-branches let a broad commitment have separate trackable components. For example, Exercise can contain Pickleball 5×/week, Strength 4×/week and Walking 5×/week, each with its own target.
How do Goals differ from Commitments?
A goal is a defined outcome with a target date and milestones. A commitment is a recurring practice. You can link commitments to goals so the daily practice supports a larger destination.
How does the chant work?
The Chant module can stream the supplied YouTube recording when online. The app does not copy that recording into the package. If the embedded player is unavailable or you are offline, select the built-in voice fallback.
Why might audio stop when my phone locks?
Mobile browsers often suspend web-page audio in the background. For uninterrupted locked-screen playback, a later native-app wrapper or licensed audio implementation would be more reliable.
How do I add my own readings or videos?
Open Wisdom, select a category, then choose Add my own resource. Paste a title, URL and short note. It remains stored on this device with the rest of your app data.
How do I install the app on my home screen?
After hosting the repository over HTTPS: on iPhone/iPad use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android use Chrome → menu → Install app / Add to Home Screen.
How do I change setup later?
Use Journal for journey settings, Commitments and Goals for their details, or come back here and choose Run setup wizard.
