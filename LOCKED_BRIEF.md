# Locked Brief — Spectacular Spirit Journal

## Final app name
**Spectacular Spirit Journal**

## Intent
A personal app for mental and physical wellness, spiritual growth, journaling, meditation, readings, goals and progress monitoring.

## Visual philosophy
Signature image: close-up crashing water with calm horizon and sunlight.
Core meaning: **rough, ragged, calm, serene, and full of light, water, and life.**

Supporting imagery:
- ocean / horizon = clarity, perspective, calm
- waves = energy, resilience, motion
- sunrise / sunset = renewal and reflection
- yellow blossoms = vitality, optimism, growth
- candlelight = stillness, spirituality, meditation

## Functional structure
- Today / Dashboard
- Journal
- Commitments
- Goals / Milestones
- Meditation
- Readings & Wisdom
- Progress / Insights

## Commitments
- at least 4 starting commitments
- minimum foundation of 2 mental + 2 physical
- user-customizable
- ability to add more commitments
- optional sub-branches under each commitment
- user-defined measures, targets and cadence

## Meditation
Three loopable options with a timer:
- chant
- generic meditative / ambient audio
- neutral soft bell soundscape

## Readings & Wisdom categories
- Spiritual
- Motivational
- Leadership
- Wellness
- Reflection / Philosophy
- Purpose / Character

Each category should include selected readings and selected video links, with the option for users to add their own resources.

## Goals
Users can define discrete goals, target dates, and milestones.

## Design principles
- structured and purposeful
- customizable
- aware but not punitive
- calm, reflective, inspiring
